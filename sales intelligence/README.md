# Sales Intelligence Workflow 

## Problem

Sales representatives were spending significant time manually summarising transcripts for sales calls.

The process involved:

- Reading long sales call transcripts
- Finding relevant information
- Summarizing transcripts
- Manually extracting notes such as pain points and identifying deal risks

This created bottlenecks and inconsistent lead quality.

## Solution

Built an AI-powered sales intelligence workflow using:

- n8n
- OpenAI
- Airtable

The workflow automatically summarises new sales call transcripts and generates actionable sales insights, inputting data into Airtable.

## Results

The workflow reduced manual transcript reading from approximately 15–20 minutes per lead to under 2 minutes of review time.

Sales representatives receive:

- Transcript summaries
- Pain points
- Next steps
- Deal risk
